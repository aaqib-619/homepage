        A festival built on crossings

        Edition Four is themed Intersection: the point where a writer meets a reader, an artist meets a passer-by, and a child meets the book that stays with them.

        From 22 to 24 January 2027, five programmes run side by side in Colombo, and a single Festival Pass opens all of them.

        04 · Five pillarsR2R3
        • All five pillars sit on the homepage, each with a one-line description, the second section after the hero. Last year's visitors didn't know the others existed, so they're never buried in the nav.
        • Shown as book spines: solid pillar colour, an Oldforge numeral and a link that filters the programme to that pillar. The same colour key runs through the slider tags, programme, speakers, menu and newsletter.
        • The Youth spine flags its own section below.

        Come for one and stay for the rest. Every pass opens all five.

          05 · YouthR2R3
          • Full-width crimson section, the youth colour, given more space than any other pillar because it's the organisers' priority.
          • Three distinct blocks: Future Writers (LKR 100,000 prize and mentorship, with a four-step path), free student passes (one tap reserves one through the same cart) and volunteering.
          • The Future Writers card is headed with the supplied newsprint texture to read as a writers' zine.

          For the next generation of storytellers

          Youth is at the heart of Edition Four: a prize that launches a writer, free entry for every student, and a place on the team that runs the festival.
