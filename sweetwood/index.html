What we found

A meaningful drop in fasting blood sugar, compared with placebo.

After 12 weeks, people taking our Ceylon cinnamon extract saw a significantly greater reduction in fasting blood sugar than people taking a placebo. The extract was also well tolerated, which is what you want from something you take every day.*

How the study worked

  1. 150 adults enrolledAdults in Sri Lanka, including people with normal, raised and high blood sugar.
  2. Split at random into two groups
    Ceylon cinnamon extractLook-alike placebo
  3. 12 weeks, taken dailyNeither participants nor researchers knew who was taking the real extract.
  4. Blood tests before and afterResearchers measured blood sugar, cholesterol and safety markers.
The fine print, in plain English
  • Participants took 1,000mg of extract a day as capsules. Each Sweet Wood shot is made with the same Ceylon cinnamon extract.
  • The main goal was to look at LDL cholesterol, which didn't change significantly. Fasting blood sugar was a secondary measure.
  • The effect on blood sugar was clearest in people whose blood sugar was already raised.
  • The study was funded by SDS Spices, Sweet Wood's parent company, and published in a peer-reviewed journal.

Not all cinnamon is the same

Most cinnamon isn't true cinnamon

The cinnamon in most supermarkets is cassia. Ceylon cinnamon, Cinnamomum verum, comes from Sri Lanka. It's gentler, more complex, and naturally very low in coumarin, a compound that can be hard on the liver in large amounts.

Questions

Good to know

What does it taste like?

Original is warm, woody and naturally sweet, like the smell of a fresh cinnamon quill. Hibiscus adds a tart, berry-like note, and Passion Fruit is bright and tropical. None of them contain added sugar.

When and how should I take it?

Shake well and drink one shot a day, ideally 10 to 20 minutes before your main meal. It tastes best chilled.

Is it safe to take every day?

Sweet Wood is made only from Ceylon cinnamon, which is naturally low in coumarin, so it's suited to daily use. The extract was well tolerated over 12 weeks in our clinical study.

It isn't suitable for children. If you're pregnant, nursing or take any medication, including for blood sugar, please speak to your doctor first.

What did the clinical study find?

In a 12-week, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial published in PLOS ONE, adults taking our Ceylon cinnamon extract saw a significantly greater reduction in fasting blood sugar than those taking a placebo. See the study summary.

Is Sweet Wood a replacement for medication?

No. Sweet Wood is a daily wellness shot, not a medicine, and it isn't intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Keep taking any medication as prescribed.

How does the subscription work?

Choose a flavour, box size and how often you'd like it. You save 15% on every delivery and shipping is free. You can switch flavours, change the date, skip a delivery or cancel anytime from your account. No commitment.

Do I need to refrigerate it?

Store unopened shots in a cool, dry place. Refrigerate after opening and enjoy chilled.
