Study details, in plain English
  • Participants took the extract as capsules, 1,000 mg a day. Sweet Wood shots are made with the same standardized extract.
  • The trial's main goal was LDL cholesterol, which did not change significantly. Fasting blood sugar was a secondary measure.
  • The difference was clearest in people whose blood sugar was already high.
  • The extract was well tolerated, with no safety concerns reported.
  • Registered with the Sri Lanka Clinical Trials Registry (SLCTR/2021/011) and funded by SDS Spices, Sweet Wood's parent company, as disclosed in the paper.

Why Ceylon

Not all cinnamon is created equal

Most cinnamon sold in the U.S. is cassia: cheaper, harsher and naturally high in coumarin, which is why health authorities publish tolerable intake limits for it. Ceylon is the other species. It's the one in our shot, and the one in our trial.

Is Sweet Wood right for you?

A cinnamon shot is new to the U.S., so here's who reaches for one, and who probably shouldn't bother.

You'll get it if you…

  • eat carb-heavy meals (rice, pasta, bread, dessert) and want daily support around them*

The honest promise: this is support, not a cure. It's the same standardized Ceylon extract that lowered fasting blood sugar in a real trial, in a format built around the one meal a day that matters most.* Take it like a habit and give it the twelve weeks the science did.

Questions

Good to know

How and when do I take it?

Shake for about ten seconds and drink one 2 oz shot a day, 15 to 20 minutes before or after your biggest, most carb-heavy meal. It tastes best chilled.

What does it taste like?

Pure Cinnamon is warm, gently spiced and clean. Hibiscus is tart and berry-like, closer to a fruit tea. Passion Fruit is bright and tropical. None of them contain added sugar.

What did the clinical study find?

In a 12-week, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial published in PLOS ONE, adults taking our standardized Ceylon cinnamon extract saw their fasting blood sugar fall by 8.59 mg/dL more, on average, than the placebo group. See the study summary.

Why Ceylon and not regular cinnamon?

Most cinnamon sold in the U.S. is cassia, which is naturally high in coumarin. Ceylon is true cinnamon from Sri Lanka: milder, subtly sweet and very low in coumarin (0.01% in our extract), so it's better suited to daily use.

Is it safe to take every day?

The extract was well tolerated over 12 weeks in our clinical trial. Sweet Wood isn't suitable for children. If you're pregnant, nursing, taking medication (including for blood sugar) or managing a health condition, talk to your healthcare provider before use.

Is Sweet Wood a replacement for medication?

No. Sweet Wood is a dietary supplement that supports healthy blood sugar. It isn't intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Keep taking any medication as prescribed.

How does the subscription work?

Choose your flavour, box size and how often you'd like it. You save 15% on every delivery and shipping is free. Switch flavours, change the date, skip a delivery or cancel anytime from your account.

How should I store it?

Store in a cool, dry place and refrigerate after opening. Shake well before use.
