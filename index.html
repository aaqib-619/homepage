Good to know

Will my jewellery tarnish?

Our pieces are 18k gold plated over stainless steel or 925 sterling silver with a protective coating, so they're made to resist tarnish through showers, workouts and swims.

Is it safe for sensitive skin?

Yes. Every piece is nickel-free and hypoallergenic.

How fast is delivery?

Order before 2pm Monday–Friday and we dispatch the same day. UK standard delivery is 1–3 working days and free over £35. Next-day delivery available at checkout.

Do you make custom jewellery?

Yes. Share your idea and a designer will send a free sketch and fixed quote within 48 hours. Custom pieces start from £65, take 2–3 weeks to make, and come in solid 9k–18k gold, gold vermeil or sterling silver with sapphires, pearls, diamonds and other gemstones.

What's your returns policy?

Free returns within 30 days on unworn items in original packaging. Personalised pieces are exchange only; custom-made pieces are final sale once you approve the design.

Can I pay later?

Yes. Split your order into 3 interest-free payments with Klarna or Clearpay at checkout.
