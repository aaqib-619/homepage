Good to Know

Do you make custom jewellery?

Yes. Share your idea and reference images, and one of our experts will guide you through metal, stones and design before anything is made.

Which metals do you work with?

18k, 22k and 24k gold, silver and platinum. Gold is available in yellow, white or rose.

Do you sell loose gemstones?

Yes. Our Gemstones collection features hand-selected loose stones, which you can buy as they are or have set in a custom design.

Can I get help choosing a piece?

Of course. Our jewellery experts can help you choose a piece, stone or metal. Chat with us on WhatsApp or book a consultation.
