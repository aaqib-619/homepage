    Your Questions, Answered

    How much does a custom piece cost?

    It depends on the metal, stones and design. Share your idea and we'll give you a clear quote before anything is made, with no obligation.

    How long does it take?

    Most pieces are completed in around three weeks once you approve the design. Let us know if you need it by a particular date.

    Can I make changes to the design?

    Yes. We refine the design with you and only begin crafting once you've approved it.

    Can you work from a photo or sketch?

    Yes. Upload reference images, sketches or screenshots with your enquiry and our experts will use them as the starting point.

    Which metals and stones can I choose?

    18k, 22k and 24k gold in yellow, white or rose, plus silver and platinum, set with sapphires, rubies, emeralds, pearls and other gemstones.
